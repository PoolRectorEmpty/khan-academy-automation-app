## 🎓 Supercharge Your Learning Journey

**Khan Academy Automation App** is a sleek, powerful desktop utility designed to streamline your educational workflow on Khan Academy. 

Whether you're a student trying to manage a heavy course load, a teacher monitoring student progress, or a lifelong learner tackling complex subjects, this tool automates the repetitive parts of the platform. It automatically aggregates your daily assignments, tracks your mastery levels across different math and science modules, and generates beautiful progress reports. 

It doesn't just save you time; it helps you learn smarter. By analyzing your performance data, the app identifies your weakest areas and curates a personalized list of practice exercises to help you improve.

> 💡 **Why use this app?** Because learning should be about understanding concepts, not clicking through menus. Let the automation handle the tracking, so you can focus on mastering the material.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

<details>
<summary><b>Does this app do my homework for me?</b></summary>

No. This app is an **analytics and tracking tool**. It helps you organize your study schedule, tracks your progress, and identifies weak areas. You still have to watch the videos and answer the questions yourself!
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Is it safe to log in?</b></summary>

Yes. The app runs completely offline and stores your credentials locally using military-grade encryption. We do not have servers, and your data is never transmitted to anyone but Khan Academy.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Will I get banned by Khan Academy?</b></summary>

The app uses standard API requests with rate-limiting to mimic human behavior. However, any automation tool carries a slight risk. We recommend using it solely for personal progress tracking.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Can I use this for multiple accounts?</b></summary>

Yes, the app supports multiple profiles, making it perfect for parents monitoring the progress of several children.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>How do I report a bug?</b></summary>

Open an issue on the GitHub repository. Include your OS version, Python version, and a screenshot of the error log.
</details>
